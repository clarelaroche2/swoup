About Swoup

Invented by a certified genius in 2020, Swoup is the world's most innovative soup distribution platform. Born out of a simple belief — that everyone deserves access to a perfect bowl of soup, wherever they are — Swoup has grown from a single pot to a nationwide phenomenon.

We partner with local chefs, home cooks, and culinary wizards to bring you an ever-rotating menu of handcrafted soups: from silky butternut squash to fiery ramen, from classic French onion to out-of-this-world lobster bisque. Every batch is made fresh, packed with care, and delivered piping hot.

Swoup isn't just soup — it's a warm hug in a bowl. It's community, it's comfort, and it's always right on time.
